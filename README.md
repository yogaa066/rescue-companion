# 🚨 Rescue Companion

### Clear actions when every minute matters.

Rescue Companion is a project I'm building for **RescueHacks 2026**.

The idea came from a simple question:

> **What if, during an emergency, you didn't have to search through the internet trying to figure out what to do next?**

When people panic, even simple decisions can become difficult. Rescue Companion is designed to make those first few minutes easier to navigate.

Instead of opening a normal chatbot and getting a long answer, the user can:

- Choose what kind of emergency is happening
- Follow a simple step-by-step emergency flow
- Quickly contact emergency services
- Alert family, friends, a trusted neighbour, or a family doctor
- Share their location
- Use an AI assistant when they're unsure what they're looking at

## 💡 The idea

The app is built around a simple flow:

**Identify → Get Help → Take Action → Stay Safe → Connect**

The goal isn't to replace doctors or emergency responders.

It's to help a person **figure out what to do next while professional help is being contacted.**

## 🚑 Initial MVP

For the first version, I'm focusing on a few common high-priority emergency situations:

- Possible heart emergency
- Stroke symptoms
- Unresponsive person
- Severe breathing difficulty
- Severe bleeding
- Choking
- Seizure
- Severe allergic reaction

Rather than trying to build a huge medical encyclopedia, I'm starting small and focusing on making each emergency flow useful and easy to follow.

## 🤖 Where AI comes in

The AI isn't meant to act like a doctor.

Its job is to understand what the user is describing and help them reach the right emergency pathway.

The actual safety-critical information will come from reliable medical and first-aid sources rather than being generated randomly by the AI.

## ⚠️ Important

This is a hackathon prototype and **not a medical or diagnostic tool**.

It should never replace emergency services, doctors, paramedics, or other healthcare professionals.
